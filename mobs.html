MOBS

ZOMBIE

Zombies are common, hostile Minecraft mobs that spawn in dark areas like caves or at night. They move slowly and attack by hitting players. While easy to defeat alone, swarms are dangerous. They burn in sunlight unless protected by water or helmets. Defeating them typically rewards players with rotten flesh.

CREEPER

Creepers are iconic, silent hostile mobs in Minecraft that ambush players by sneaking up and exploding. Unlike zombies, they do not burn in sunlight, making them a constant daytime threat. They are famously terrified of cats and ocelots. Defeating them before they detonate rewards players with valuable gunpowder.

SKELETON

Skeletons are common hostile mobs in Minecraft armed with bows, attacking players from a distance with deadly accuracy. Like zombies, they spawn in dark areas and burn in direct sunlight unless wearing a helmet or standing in water. Defeating them yields arrows, bones, and occasionally their bows or armor.

ENDERMAN

Endermen are tall, neutral Minecraft mobs with glowing purple eyes that spawn in all three dimensions. They peacefully wander and move blocks unless a player attacks them or looks directly into their faces, which triggers a relentless, teleporting attack. Defeating them yields Ender Pearls, essential for late-game travel and progression.

WARDEN

The Warden is a terrifying, blind hostile mob summoned by Sculk Shriekers in the underground Deep Dark biome. It cannot see, instead tracking players through sound vibrations and smell. Boasting massive health and a wall-penetrating sonic boom attack, it is designed to be sneaked around rather than fought.

WITHER

The Wither is a destructive, three-headed undead boss in Minecraft, manually summoned by players using soul sand and wither skeleton skulls. It flies and aggressively bombards targets with explosive projectiles that inflict a deadly, health-draining decay effect. Defeating this formidable foe rewards the rare Nether Star, essential for crafting beacons.
